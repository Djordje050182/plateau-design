A new home for Plateau Design, built around your work.

Baz, this is a working concept, not a finished product. Everything on it is your own content, restructured and redesigned. Have a look on your phone as well as a laptop: djordje050182.github.io/plateau-design

What the concept does

  • Keeps your identity, sharpened. The boxed PLATEAU wordmark and your teal remain, so it still reads as your studio rather than a template.
  • Leads with the work. Your project photography runs full width, with all six fields of work shown as a proper portfolio grid instead of background wallpaper.
  • Tells one story. Statement, studio, work, services, experience, contact. A council client or developer can size you up in one scroll.
  • Puts you front and centre. Fifteen years, Context, Place Design Group, Principal at RPS. That experience is the reason to hire a new studio, so it has its own section with you on site.
  • Handles the plumbing. Mobile optimised, fast single page, acknowledgement of Country, and structured data so Google and AI assistants describe the studio accurately.

Ideas to take it further

  • Project case studies. Three to five flagship projects, each with a name, location, your role and a short set of photos. This is the single biggest upgrade.
  • Full colour originals. The current images are compressed pulls from the old site. Your best high resolution photography would transform it.
  • Real captions. Project names and locations under every image build credibility and help search.
  • Client voices. One or two short testimonials from council or developer clients.
  • A short notes section. Occasional pieces on planting, play design or LVIA. Good for search rankings and it positions you as the expert you are.
  • Contact form. Currently phone and email links. A simple form is an easy add if you prefer enquiries that way.

Next steps if you like it

  1. Send over full colour photo sets and project names, plus anything you cannot show publicly.
  2. Point plateaudesign.com.au at the new site. Hosting is free, so the Squarespace subscription can go.
  3. Add the case studies and a contact form.
  4. Set up analytics so you can see who is visiting.

Nothing here is fixed. Type, colours, layout, wording and photography can all change. Treat it as a starting point to react to.
